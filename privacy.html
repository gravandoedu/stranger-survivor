Privacy Policy

Stranger Survivor Publisher · Last updated: October 31, 2026.

This Policy explains what data Stranger Survivor Publisher uses, why, and how it is stored. The app is operated by Stranger Survivor.

1. Data the app uses

  • TikTok access token, received when you authorize the app through TikTok's official login.
  • Basic information about the authorized profile, needed to confirm which account will receive the video.
  • Videos, captions and publishing settings chosen by you.

2. Why the data is used

The data is used only to authenticate the authorized account and upload the videos you choose to TikTok, with the caption and privacy settings you define.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens, videos and logs are stored on the operator's computer in local files. The app does not keep a cloud server with this data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for advertising. Videos and captions are transmitted to TikTok's API because that is required to publish content to the authorized account. TikTok's handling of data is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy.

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics or advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the authorization remains active. You may revoke access in TikTok's settings and request deletion of local data using the contact below. Deletion does not remove content already published on TikTok.

6. Security

Access to the files is restricted to the operator's computer. No method is completely secure, but the app avoids exposing tokens on public pages and does not send credentials to unrelated third parties.

7. Your rights

You may request information, correction or deletion of your personal data under applicable law, including the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD). Use the contact below.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not intentionally collect data from children.

9. Changes

This Policy may be updated. The current version is always the one published on this page, with the update date shown above.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: gravandoedu@gmail.com.
